# Delivery record

## Outcome and scope

State the requested outcome, exclusions, current stage and relevant specification paths.

## Current state

Describe observed behaviour and the revision inspected. Distinguish proposals and assumptions.

## Decisions and gates

Record decisions, applicable constraints, authorization already given and unresolved questions.

## Acceptance and evidence

| Criterion | Expected behaviour | Implementation | Check and environment | Observed result | Gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Include dated commands, test counts, skips and evidence locations. Keep local, CI and deployment results separate.

## Next work

List the next authorized steps, blockers and relevant recovery instructions. Remove stale status and sensitive information before sharing.

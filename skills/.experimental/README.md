# Experimental skills

This classification is for skills being developed or evaluated before they are ready for curated use. They give contributors and maintainers a place to gather feedback on a real workflow, test instructions and identify dependencies or compatibility limitations.

Experimental skills must follow the [skill standard](../../docs/SKILL_STANDARD.md), clearly state their maturity and known limitations, and have names unique across all tiers. Their behaviour and support may change as evidence is gathered; users should review each skill and its requirements before use.

Promotion to curated status requires the same review as a new curated contribution, including ownership, provenance, dependencies and representative verification. See the [catalogue design](../../docs/CATALOGUE.md) for the tier criteria.

No experimental skills have been published yet.
